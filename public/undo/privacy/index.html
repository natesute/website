Privacy policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

Undo is a knot puzzle game for iPhone made by Nathan Suttie. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

No account, no servers of mine

You don't sign up or log in to play. Your progress, settings and streaks are stored on your phone and in your phone's own backups. I don't run any server that receives your game data, and I can't see it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: an occasional ad between levels, and an optional ad you can watch to skip an Endless knot. To show and measure ads, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • device and advertising identifiers, such as Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking
  • your approximate location, worked out from your IP address
  • information about the ads you see and tap, and how the app is used
  • diagnostic information such as crashes and performance

The app asks for permission before allowing tracking across other companies' apps and websites. If you say no, you still get ads, but they aren't personalised using that identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before any ad, and you can choose how your data is used.

Google handles this information under its own policies. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google privacy policy.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and I never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether you own the purchase, so it can turn ads off and restore the purchase on a new phone.

Children

Undo isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Turn off tracking in your iPhone's Settings, as above.
  • Buy "Remove ads" to stop the ads between levels.
  • Delete the app to remove all game data stored on your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
